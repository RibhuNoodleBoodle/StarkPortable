1. Follow a small hint and a subtitle all the way to the LCD

In engines/stark/services/fontprovider.cpp, FontHolder reads gui.ini or
uses defaults such as 12 for small Garamond and 19 for big Florentine
Script. It derives _scaledHeight from the screen viewport and requests a
FreeType cell height. In engines/stark/visual/text.cpp, createBitmap wraps
at a scaled width, draws white glyphs to an RGBA surface, extracts coverage,
calls blendWithColor and multiplyColorWithAlpha, then uploads a nearest-
filtered bitmap. VisualText already requests grid snapping and a no-scaling
override. In gfx/pspgu.cpp, mapLogical2DRectToRt still scales the bitmap
extent by render-target viewport / current viewport with that override.
At a full 640x480 viewport, a 12-pixel logical cell occupies only six scene
rows before accounting for the font's own ascent, descent and whitespace.
The final LCD blit in backends/platform/psp/display_manager.cpp uses linear
filtering. Grid snapping alone cannot recover a stem lost in the first draw.

Capture the actual font, cell height, wrap width, bitmap and drawn-quad
dimensions for the two strings. Inspect the glyph mask, the 320x240 scene
and the final LCD. Check thin strokes at adjacent logical origins and on
both light and dark backgrounds. Trace partial coverage through the two
color helpers, GU premultiplied blending, and pspGuClassifySurfacePsm for
long text. Establish which stage loses detail before changing it.

3. Repair and verify ownership

* Rejected font load. FontHolder creates a MemoryReadStream borrowing the
  bytes in _ttfDataCache and passes it to Graphics::loadTTFFont with
  DisposeAfterUse::YES. In graphics/fonts/ttf.cpp, TTFFont takes ownership
  only on successful initialization. The factory returns null on failure,
  and FontHolder loses the wrapper. Follow the failure handling in
  loadTTFFontFromArchive. Retain a local owner until success, release the
  wrapper exactly once on failure, and preserve the default-font fallback.
  Test a present but rejected TTF repeatedly as well as a valid one. A
  missing file exercises a different branch. Keep the cached bytes alive
  until all borrowing faces close and keep the disk handles short-lived.

* Failed dialog background decode. In engines/stark/ui/dialogbox.cpp,
  DialogBox::loadBackground allocates bitmapWithHeader with new[], wraps
  it in a non-owning MemoryReadStream, and returns early if BitmapDecoder
  rejects it. The delete[] runs only on success. Give the temporary bytes
  a scoped owner with a matching deallocator. Validate the size and read
  count before decoding. Preserve the solid-color fallback. Exercise both
  valid and deliberately truncated data using a private copy or a fixture.

* Follow lifetime through font reinitialization, VisualText replacement,
  glyph caches, temporary raster surfaces, PSP bitmap tiles and fade masks,
  menu close, room changes and return to launcher. Preserve GE fencing.
  For each further leak identified, record the allocation, missing release,
  trigger and evidence after the fix. Distinguish a reusable font or glyph
  cache from per-entry growth. Reuse bounded allocations where appropriate.
